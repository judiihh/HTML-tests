# 💗 Pink Website Practice

Welcome to **Judith's Cute Pink Corner** – a playful practice website built to experiment with HTML, CSS, and a sprinkle of JavaScript magic. This repo holds the `HTML-tests/Practice Web/PinkWebsite.html` file that powers the adorable experience. 🌸

## ✨ Highlights
- **Soft pink gradients** and sparkly accents that set a cozy, friendly tone.
- **Feature cards** that open modals for "My Story" and "My Why's", sharing personal motivation.
- **Floating hearts** animation for delightful, interactive clicks. 💕
- **Mini gallery** with polaroid-style placeholders to swap with your own images or quotes.
- **Contact section** featuring quick links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, and email – perfect for staying connected. 💌

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Open the repository folder in your editor of choice.
2. Launch the webpage by opening `HTML-tests/Practice Web/PinkWebsite.html` in your browser (double-click or drag into the browser window).
3. Click around to experience the modals, toggle sparkles, and watch the floating hearts appear.

## 🔧 Customization Ideas
- Update the copy inside the modals to tell your own story.
- Swap the emoji placeholders in the gallery with your own artwork or photos.
- Adjust the color variables at the top of the `<style>` block to explore new palettes.
- Add more feature cards or social buttons to grow the layout.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5** for semantic structure.
- **CSS3** (inline `<style>` block) for responsive, polished visuals.
- **Vanilla JavaScript** to handle modal behavior and cute heart animations.

Enjoy crafting your own pink-perfect web moments! 🌷
